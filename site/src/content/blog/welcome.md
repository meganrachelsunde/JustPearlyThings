For those of you that don't know: I was kicked off of all platforms and demonetized for two
years. Essentially unemployed. What we're doing to prevent that from ever mattering again is
this — a website that we own, so nobody can kick us off.

Here's what you'll find:

- **[Donate](/donate)** — every way to support the network, and exactly what we do with the
  money: content first, then legal defense, travel and filming, and the fight against family
  court corruption.
- **[Events](/events)** — we're going to do events once a month to raise money. Announcements
  land there and on the email list first.
- **[Resources for fathers](/resources)** — organizations and legal aid that can actually help
  if you're in the middle of it.
- **[Statistics & sources](/statistics)** — the numbers behind the show, every claim with a
  link to the primary source.
- **[Watch](/watch)** — every place the show lives, including Terrence's series on
  administrative violence.

In the future we'd like to make an app — but everything costs money, you know what I mean? If
you want to get involved, the links are everywhere on this site. And if you work in nonprofits
or marketing and have tips, reach out.
